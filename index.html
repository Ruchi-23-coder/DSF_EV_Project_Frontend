About this dashboard

This is a static front-end dashboard. All results were produced in Google Colab and are embedded here as fixed values. It is not a live, database-connected application, and it does not recompute or fetch data.

Objectives

  • Measure global EV stock and sales growth, 2010–2025.
  • Compare countries by EV stock and public charging points (2024).
  • Test how closely EV stock and charging infrastructure move together.
  • Track sustainability indicators: electricity demand, oil displacement, battery deployment.
  • Compare 2035 EV stock under the CPS and STEPS scenarios.
  • Group 43 countries with K-Means clustering.

Dataset source

File: EV data by country 2026.xlsx, main sheet GEVO_EV_2026 (Global EV Outlook 2026 data). 49,375 rows × 9 columns: region_country, category, parameter, mode, powertrain, year, unit, value, Aggregate group. Years 2010–2035; 72 countries/regions; 19 parameters.

Official source: International Energy Agency (IEA), Global EV Outlook 2026.

Methodology

  1. Loaded sheet GEVO_EV_2026 with pandas.
  2. Checked missing values: unit 22,582 and region_country 1,078, all in price parameters (not used in the analysis).
  3. Removed 15 exact duplicates → 49,360 rows.
  4. Global series: Aggregate group = _World, category = Historical, powertrain = EV.
  5. Country series (2024): groups Other + Projection_country; modes Cars, 2 and 3 wheelers, Vans, Buses, Trucks summed.
  6. Merged EV stock and charging points: 43 countries. Pearson and Spearman correlation.
  7. Projections: category Projection-CPS / Projection-STEPS, year 2035, World.
  8. K-Means (k=2): log1p transform → StandardScaler → KMeans(random_state=42, n_init=10).

Key findings and conclusion

    Notebook-level cautions: EV sales and stock share are simple averages across the five vehicle modes, not true fleet-wide shares. Country totals include 2 and 3 wheelers, which is why India and Viet Nam rank high.
